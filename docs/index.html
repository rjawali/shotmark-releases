What it does

Everything you used Loom and a screenshot tool for, in one app that lives in your menu bar.

Capture anything

An area, a window, a whole screen or a scrolling page, each with its own shortcut.

Record like Loom

Narrate a walkthrough with a camera bubble, or record a GIF; press Stop and the Drive link is in your clipboard.

Mark it up

Arrows, comments, numbered steps, blur and redact, crop and text recognition in one editor.

Stays out of the way

Pin a capture to your screen, set your own shortcuts, and let ShotMark update itself.

The markup editor

After every capture

A small panel confirms the save without pulling you out of your work: Copy, Edit, Save As, Pin to screen, Share, or put a Google Drive link in your clipboard.

Install in three steps

About a minute. The installer is signed and notarized, so macOS opens it without warnings.

ShotMark.dmg
1

Open the download

Click ShotMark.dmg in your browser’s downloads, or find it in your Downloads folder.

2

Drag ShotMark into Applications

Drop the icon on the Applications folder. If you open it from the installer instead, ShotMark offers to move itself.

3

Open ShotMark and allow screen capture

macOS asks once for Screen Recording; the welcome walks you through it and your first shot.

Yours, on your Mac

There is no ShotMark server. The only connections are GitHub for updates and Google for your own Drive.

Signed and notarized

Built for Apple silicon and Intel, macOS 13 or later, and notarized by Apple.

Files stay on your Mac

Captures save to your Pictures folder; videos you share go to your own Google Drive. No telemetry.

Updates itself

New versions arrive in the app; install this once and you are done.
